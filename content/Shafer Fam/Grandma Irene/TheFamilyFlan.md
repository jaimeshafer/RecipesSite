### Ingredients

- 1 Yellow cake mix
- 1 8oz cream cheese
- Can cherry pie filling
- 1 Packet dream whip

### Instructions
- Pre-heated oven to 350
- Grease flan pan generously
- Follow directions on cake mix box
- Pour about 1/2 - 3/4 of mix into pan. Meanwhile soften cream cheese at room temp in small bowl.
- Bake cake about 25 min (check with tooth pick)
- Cool on rack about 15 min, then turn over on rack and cool completely.
- Follow dream whip directions. When thick, whip cream cheese until smooth and add 1/4th cup granulated sugar and whip together. Add to dream whip.
- Lastly, add to cake and add cherry topping.
