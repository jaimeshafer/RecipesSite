# Ingredients

- 1/2 Cup sugar - granulated
- 1/2 Cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 Cup peanut butter - creamy
- 1/2 Cup butter - softened
- 1 Egg
- 1 1/2 Cups flour - all-purpose
- 3/4 Teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 Teaspoon baking powder
- 36 Hershey's Kisses

# Steps:

- Pre-heat oven to 375
- Large bowl-beat sugar, brown sugar, peanut butter, butter, & egg on medium.
- Stir in flour, baking soda, and baking powder till dough forms.
- Shape dough into 1-inch balls; roll in extra sugar.
- Place 2-inches apart on ungreased cookie sheets.
- Bake 8-10 minutes (edges light golden brown)
- Immediately press 1 Hershey's Kiss in the center of each cookie.
