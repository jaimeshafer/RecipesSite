## Meat

- USDA Choice
- ~8.5 lb full packer
- Minimal trim

## Seasoning

- 8:00 PM
- Meat Church Holy Cow
- Light layer Meat Church Holy Gospel

## Fuel

- Meat Church Pellets

## Cook Timeline

### 8:00 PM

- Seasoned and refrigerated uncovered

### 12:00 AM

- On smoker
- 225°F
- Fat side down

### 8:05 AM

- Bark fully set
- Wrapped in butcher paper
- Two sheets:
  - First lengthwise
  - Second crosswise
- Returned fat side down

### 8:45 AM

- Smoker 235°F

### 9:15 AM

- Smoker 240°F

### 10:10 AM

- Smoker 250°F

### 10:53 AM

- Internal ~172°F

### 12:30 PM

- Internal ~188°F

### 12:55 PM

- Began probing
- Flat still resisted
- Continued cooking

### 1:30 PM

- Flat noticeably smoother
- Continued cooking

### ~1:50 PM

- Flat probe tender
- Point ~210°F
- Middle ~202°F
- Pulled from smoker

## Rest

- Vented in butcher paper about 15 minutes
- Held in oven Keep Warm at 155°F
- Rested until approximately 4:15 PM

## Lessons Learned

- Wrap based on bark, not temperature.
- Probe through butcher paper is perfectly acceptable.
- Flat determines when brisket is done.
- Ignore 195°F if flat still resists.
- 250°F after wrapping worked very well.
- Long hold at 155°F was excellent.
- Two-sheet butcher paper wrap worked fine.
- Minimal trim worked, but trim a little more next time for more even cooking.
- Continue slicing around 1/4 inch.
