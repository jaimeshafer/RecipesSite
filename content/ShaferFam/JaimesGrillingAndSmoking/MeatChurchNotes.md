## Holy Cow

- Preferred beef/brisket base.
- Excellent coarse bark.

## Holy Gospel

- Excellent secondary layer.
- Works on brisket and chicken.
- Adds color and complexity without overwhelming the primary rub.

## Texas Sugar

- Favorite rib rub.
- Also successfully tested on wings.
- Good bark and color.

## Honey Hog

- Excellent chicken base.
- Used in wrapped ribs.
- Sweet without excessive heat.

## Honey Hog Hot

- Mild/moderate heat.
- Generally approachable for family.

## Holy Voodoo

- Excellent on wings.
- Savory/spicy profile.

## Current Favorite Combinations

### Brisket

- Holy Cow + light Holy Gospel

### Whole Chicken

- Honey Hog + light Holy Gospel

### Ribs

- Texas Sugar + light Holy Gospel

### Wings

- Texas Sugar **or** Holy Voodoo
