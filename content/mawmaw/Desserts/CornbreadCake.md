## Ingredients
- 1 cup sugar  
- 1 cup brown sugar  
- 4 eggs  
- 1 cup oil  
- 1 ½ cups self-rising flour  
- 1 tsp vanilla  
- 1 cup pecans  

## Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients together.  
2. Pour into a greased, lightly floured 9x13 pan.  
3. Bake at **325°F** for 50 minutes.
