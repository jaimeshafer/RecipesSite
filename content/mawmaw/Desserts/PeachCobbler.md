## Ingredients
- 1 cup sugar  
- 1 cup milk  
- 1 cup flour  
- 1 stick butter  
- 1 can (29 oz) peaches (may substitute blueberries)  

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.  
2. In a bowl, mix together sugar, milk, and flour.  
3. Place butter in a casserole dish and melt it in the oven.  
4. Pour the sugar–milk–flour mixture into the melted butter **without stirring**.  
5. Add the peaches (or blueberries) on top **without stirring**.  
6. Bake until golden brown.  
7. Serve warm.
