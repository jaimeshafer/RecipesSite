## Ingredients
- 1 ¼ pounds shrimp, cleaned and cooked  
- 1 large onion, chopped  
- 1 tablespoon margarine  
- 1 can mushroom soup  
- ½ tablespoon lemon juice  
- Dash of garlic salt  
- Salt and pepper, to taste  
- ¾ cup cooked rice  
- ½ cup commercial sour cream  
- ¾ cup grated cheese  
- ½ green pepper, sliced  

## Preparation
1. Cook and clean the shrimp.  
2. Sauté the chopped onion in margarine until tender.  
3. Make the sauce: combine mushroom soup, lemon juice, garlic salt, salt, and pepper.  
4. Fold the cooked rice and shrimp into the sauce.  
5. Fold in the sour cream.  

## Cooking
1. Pour the mixture into a 1-quart buttered baking dish.  
2. Sprinkle grated cheese on top and decorate with green pepper rings (parboil green pepper for 2 minutes before adding).  
3. Bake at 325°F (165°C) for 30 minutes.  

## Serving
- Serves 6.  
- Serve hot as a main dish or with a light side salad.  

**Tip:** Parboiling the green pepper ensures it stays tender but retains some crunch and color.
