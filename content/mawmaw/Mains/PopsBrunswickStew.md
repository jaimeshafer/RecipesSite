## Ingredients
- 2 ½ lb. pork BBQ  
- 2 lb. beef roast (cook in crock pot & save strained stock)  
- 2 ½ lb. whole chicken (remove skin & debone)  
- 3 cans tomato soup  
- 3 cans whole tomatoes (chopped)  
- 2 large sweet onions (finely chopped)  
- 2 cans whole sweet corn  
- 1 can cream corn  
- 2 cups small lima beans (cook and puree)  
- 8 oz. bottle Worcestershire sauce  
- 6 red pepper pods (finely chopped)  
- 1 stick butter (¼ lb.)  
- 1 small bottle "spicy" BBQ sauce  
- 1 oz. "Whitts" vinegar sauce  
- 1 oz. "Whitts" HOT sauce (use 2 oz. for more heat)  
- Salt and black pepper, to taste  
- Cayenne pepper, to taste (add last, after tasting)  

## Preparation (Day Before)
1. Grate the beef and chicken along with ⅔ of the pork.  
2. Shred the remaining ⅓ of pork.  
3. Mix all meats together.  
4. Add 2 cups beef stock and 2 cups chicken broth.  
5. Store in fridge overnight.  

## Cooking (Next Day)
1. In a large stock pot, add corn, onion, tomatoes, and red pepper pods.  
   - Cook slowly at a low boil for ~1 hour, stirring often.  
2. Add pureed limas, Worcestershire sauce, spicy BBQ sauce, butter, vinegar sauce, hot sauce, salt, and black pepper.  
   - Simmer for 30 minutes.  
3. Stir in meats. Simmer until hot, stirring often.  
   - Do not let stew stick.  
4. Taste and adjust heat with cayenne pepper.  

**Tip:** If you like it spicier, add more Whitts hot sauce or extra cayenne at the end.
