### Ingredients
- 1 whole chicken
- Noodles
- Salt and pepper
- Extra broth (if needed)

### Instructions:
1. Cook whole chicken until done.  
2. Use the broth from the chicken to cook the noodles.  
3. Debone the chicken when done.  
4. Cook noodles, adding chicken as noodles finish cooking.  
5. Season with salt and pepper.  
6. Add extra broth if you do not have enough.
