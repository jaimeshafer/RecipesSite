  ## Ingredients
- 4 to 5 pieces cube steak  
- Flour (for dredging and gravy)  
- Oil  
- Water  

## Instructions
1. Cut steak pieces in half.  
2. Dredge each piece in flour.  
3. Heat oil in a skillet on medium (not high).  
4. When oil is hot, fry both sides of steak until browned (do not cook through).  
5. Transfer browned steak pieces to a crockpot or electric skillet.  
6. In the same skillet, use the remaining flour to make a gravy:  
   - Brown flour to a medium color.  
   - Add water until it reaches gravy consistency.  
   - Stir until it starts to thicken.  
7. Pour gravy over the meat in the crockpot or electric skillet.  
8. Cook on **low** for about 3 hours.
