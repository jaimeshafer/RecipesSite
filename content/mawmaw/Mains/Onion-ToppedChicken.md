## Ingredients
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breast halves  
- 4 medium potatoes, peeled and halved  
- 1 can (10 ¾ oz) condensed cream of chicken soup, undiluted  
- 1 cup sour cream  
- 1 can (2.8 oz) French-fried onions  

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.  
2. Place chicken in a greased 9-inch square baking dish.  
3. Arrange potatoes around the chicken.  
4. In a bowl, combine soup and sour cream; spread over chicken and potatoes.  
5. Bake uncovered for 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes.  
6. Sprinkle with French-fried onions and bake 10 minutes longer.  
7. Serve hot.  

*Serves 4*
