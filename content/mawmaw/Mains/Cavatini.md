### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 small oninon, chopped
- 1 jar of your favorite spoghetti sauce
- 1 small can chopped mushrooms (optional)
- 1 3-ounce package pepperoni
- 1/2 package italian trio noodles
- 2 cups grated mozzarella cheese

### Instructions
1. Brown meat with onion and drain
2. Cook noodles according to package instructions and drain
3. Mix all ingredients except cheese and pour into 9x13 inch cassrole dish, top with cheese
4. Bake at 350 degrees for 15-20 minutes
5. Serve with salad and galic bread
