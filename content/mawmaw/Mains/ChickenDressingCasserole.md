### Ingredients
- 1 pkg. Pepperidge Farm herbed stuffing mix
- 1 small chopped onion
- 1 stick margarine or butter
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 can cream of celery soup
- 1 can chicken broth
- 1 whole cooked, deboned chicken

### Instructions:
1. Melt butter in a pan and sauté onion.
2. Add onion to stuffing in a bowl and set aside.
3. Place ½ the stuffing in a greased casserole dish.
4. Layer ½ the chicken over stuffing.
5. Mix soups and chicken broth together, pour ½ over chicken.
6. Repeat layers.
7. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
