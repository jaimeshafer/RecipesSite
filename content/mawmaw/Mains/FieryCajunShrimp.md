## Ingredients
- 2 sticks (½ lb) butter, melted  
- 2 sticks (½ lb) margarine, melted  
- 3–4 oz (⅜ to ½ cup) Worcestershire sauce  
- 4 tbsp ground black pepper  
- 1 tsp ground rosemary  
- 2 tsp Tabasco  
- 2 tsp salt (sea salt preferred)  
- 3 cloves garlic, minced  
- 4 lemons (2 juiced, 2 sliced)  
- 5–6 lbs raw shrimp, in shells  

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.  
2. In a large bowl, mix together all ingredients except lemon slices and shrimp.  
3. Pour about ½ cup of sauce into the bottom of a large baking dish.  
4. Arrange alternating layers of shrimp and lemon slices until the dish is nearly full, leaving 1 inch of headroom.  
5. Pour remaining sauce evenly over shrimp and lemon slices.  
6. Bake uncovered at 400°F for 15–20 minutes, stirring once or twice, until shrimp are cooked through.
