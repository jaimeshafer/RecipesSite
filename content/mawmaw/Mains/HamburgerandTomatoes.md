## Ingredients
- 1 lb hamburger  
- 1 can stewed tomatoes  
- 1 small onion, sliced  
- Oil  
- Seasonings of your choice  

## Instructions
1. Form hamburger into patties and season as desired.  
2. Heat oil in a skillet and fry patties until browned on one side.  
3. Turn patties to brown the other side.  
4. Place a slice of onion on top of each patty.  
5. Pour the can of stewed tomatoes over the patties.  
6. Reduce heat and simmer for at least 30 minutes.
