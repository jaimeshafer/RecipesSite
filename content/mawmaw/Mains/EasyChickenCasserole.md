## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice (uncooked)  
- 1 cup water  
- 1 can cream of chicken soup  
- 1 can cream of celery soup  
- 4 chicken breasts  
- ½ stick butter, melted  
- Salt & pepper, to taste  

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 325°F.  
2. In a bowl, mix cream of chicken soup, cream of celery soup, rice, and water.  
3. Melt butter and season lightly with salt & pepper.  
4. Roll chicken breasts in melted butter and lightly sauté.  
5. Pour soup and rice mixture into a casserole dish.  
6. Place sautéed chicken breasts on top.  
7. Pour remaining melted butter over casserole.  
8. Bake uncovered at 325°F for 45 minutes.
