## Ingredients
- 1 small head cabbage  
- ½ small onion  
- ½ teaspoon vinegar  
- ¾ cup mayonnaise (may need more)  
- Salt and pepper, to taste  

## Preparation
1. Shred the cabbage and dice the onion.  
2. In a bowl, combine cabbage, onion, vinegar, and mayonnaise.  
3. Season with salt and pepper to taste.  
4. Mix thoroughly and cover.  

## Serving
- Refrigerate for several hours before serving for best flavor.  
- Serve as a side dish with sandwiches, fried foods, or barbecues.  

**Tip:** Adjust the mayonnaise to reach your preferred creaminess.
