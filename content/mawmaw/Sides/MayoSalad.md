## Ingredients
- 1 large head lettuce  
- 1 cucumber, quartered into small pieces  
- ½ onion, chopped  
- ½ pkg bacon bits  
- 1 tomato, chopped  
- ½ pkg shredded cheddar cheese  
- Mayonnaise (enough to coat)  

## Instructions
1. Combine lettuce, cucumber, onion, bacon bits, tomato, and cheese in a large bowl.  
2. About 30 minutes before serving, add mayonnaise.  
3. Mix until salad is lightly coated (adjust mayo to taste).  
4. Serve chilled.
