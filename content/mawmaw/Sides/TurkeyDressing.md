## Ingredients
- 4 cups bread crumbs  
- 2 cups un-popped popcorn  
- 1 teaspoon salt  
- 2 tablespoons sage  

## Preparation
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).  
2. In a large bowl, mix together bread crumbs, un-popped popcorn, salt, and sage.  
3. Spread the mixture in a baking dish.  

## Cooking
- Bake in the preheated oven until the popcorn pops and the dressing is lightly browned, stirring occasionally if desired.  

## Serving
- Serve as a side dish with turkey or other holiday meals.  

**Tip:** Keep an eye on the popcorn—oven temperatures may vary, and you don’t want it to burn!
