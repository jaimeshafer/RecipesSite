## Ingredients
- Leftover creamy potatoes  
- ¼ onion, chopped  
- 1 egg  
- 1 tablespoon flour  
- Oil (for frying)  
- Optional: 1 tablespoon milk (if mixture is too thick)  

## Preparation
1. In a bowl, mix the leftover creamy potatoes, chopped onion, egg, and flour.  
2. If the mixture is too thick, stir in 1 tablespoon of milk.  

## Cooking
1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat.  
2. Drop the potato mixture by tablespoonfuls into the skillet (about 4 at a time).  
3. Cook each side until golden brown.  

## Serving
- Serve hot as a side dish or snack.  

**Tip:** Don’t overcrowd the skillet; cook in batches for even browning.
