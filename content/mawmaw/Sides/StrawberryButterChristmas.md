## Ingredients
- 2 sticks real butter  
- 1 cup powdered sugar  
- 1 (10 oz) package frozen strawberries  

## Preparation
1. Combine all ingredients in a blender or food processor.  
2. Blend until smooth and fully combined.  

## Serving & Storage
- Transfer to a decorative jar if desired.  
- Serve on bread, muffins, or scones.  
- Store in the refrigerator.  

**Tip:** Let the strawberries thaw slightly for easier blending.
