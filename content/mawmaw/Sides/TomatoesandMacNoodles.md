## Ingredients
- 2 tomatoes (stewed or canned)  
- 1 small box macaroni noodles  

## Preparation
1. Cook macaroni noodles according to package directions.  
2. Drain any excess water once noodles are cooked.  

## Cooking
1. Pour stewed tomatoes over the cooked noodles.  
2. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for approximately 30 minutes.  

## Serving
- Serve hot as a simple side dish or main course.  

**Tip:** Use fresh stewed tomatoes for a richer flavor, or season with a pinch of salt and pepper to taste.
