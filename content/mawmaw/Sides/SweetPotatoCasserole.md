## Ingredients

### Casserole
- 3 cups mashed sweet potatoes  
- 1 cup sugar  
- 2 eggs  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla  
- ½ cup melted butter  
- ½ cup milk  

### Topping
- 1 cup brown sugar  
- 1 cup chopped pecans  
- 1/3 cup flour  
- 1/3 cup melted butter  

## Preparation

### Casserole
1. In a bowl, mix mashed sweet potatoes, sugar, eggs, vanilla, melted butter, and milk using a mixer until smooth.  
2. Pour mixture into a casserole dish.  

### Topping
1. In a separate bowl, combine brown sugar, chopped pecans, flour, and melted butter.  
2. Mix with a fork, then crumble over the top of the sweet potato mixture using your fingers.  

## Cooking
- Bake in a preheated oven at 350°F (175°C) for about 20 minutes, until the topping is lightly browned.  

## Serving
- Serve warm as a side dish for holiday meals or family dinners.  

**Tip:** For extra flavor, toast the pecans lightly before adding them to the topping.
