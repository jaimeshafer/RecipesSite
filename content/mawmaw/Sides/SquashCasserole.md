## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked yellow squash, drained  
- 1 can mushroom soup  
- 1 onion, cut into small pieces  
- ½ cup margarine (1 stick), melted  
- 2 cups grated cheese  
- 3 eggs, beaten  
- Salt and pepper, to taste  
- ½ cup crushed Ritz crackers  

## Preparation
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).  
2. In a bowl, mix cooked squash, mushroom soup, onion, melted margarine, grated cheese, eggs, salt, and pepper.  
3. Grease or spray a baking pan with vegetable spray.  
4. Pour the mixture into the prepared pan.  
5. Sprinkle crushed Ritz crackers evenly on top.  

## Cooking
- Bake in the preheated oven for 25–40 minutes, until the casserole is set and the top is lightly browned.  

## Serving
- Serve hot as a side dish.  

**Tip:** Ensure the squash is well drained to prevent a watery casserole.
